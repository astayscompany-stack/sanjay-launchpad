# Full-Screen Landing Concept Previews

## Goal
Make each “Open proposed layout” action open a complete landing-page preview rather than an embedded card, with its own sky-blue visual environment and scrolling.

## Experience
- Replace the proposal content area with a full-width concept preview when a direction is opened.
- Give the preview its own fixed top bar with a clear back control and concept position.
- Make the landing page itself independently scroll from top to bottom inside the available screen area.
- Hide proposal navigation while viewing a concept so it feels like opening the proposed website itself.
- Use a fresh sky-blue background treatment across all three concepts while preserving ZHEP’s teal brand accent and readable content.
- Expand each concept into a complete page flow with additional relevant sections and a closing call-to-action.

## Responsive behavior
- On desktop, use the full proposal workspace beneath the preview bar.
- On mobile, use the entire viewport without the proposal bottom navigation covering the page.
- Keep the preview bar visible while the proposed page scrolls.

## Quality checks
- Verify all three concepts open, scroll to their final section, and return correctly.
- Check at 478×885 and 1280×900.
- Confirm no clipped content, missing images, console errors, or build errors.
