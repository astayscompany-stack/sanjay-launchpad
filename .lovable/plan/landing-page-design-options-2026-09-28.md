# Landing Page Design Options

## Goal
Add a clickable design gallery inside the Landing Page proposal view so Sanjay can compare proposed ZHEP landing-page directions without leaving the command center.

## Experience
- Keep the existing one-route app navigation.
- In Landing Page, show three visual concept cards derived from the supplied ZHEP material.
- Opening a concept replaces the proposal content with a detailed, responsive landing-page mockup and a clear back control.
- Each mockup uses a focused subset of supplied imagery plus selected product, wellness, and partnership facts—not an image dump.

## Concepts
1. Wellness-first brand story: preventive health, balance, Ayurvedic heritage, and liver-awareness content.
2. Product discovery: curated kits, Family Pack, Zealbeats, and category-led browsing.
3. Partner growth: customer partnership model, four-step joining journey, and program benefits without commercial figures.

## Quality
- Preserve the proposal’s teal, charcoal, and light design system.
- Ensure mobile bottom navigation never obscures content.
- Maintain accessible buttons, keyboard operation, concise plain-language content, and reduced-motion support.
- Verify at 478×885 and 1280×900, then check diagnostics.
